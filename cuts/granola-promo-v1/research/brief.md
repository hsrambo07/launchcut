## Positioning

**Granola is the AI notepad for people in back-to-back meetings: you write the notes you want, Granola fills in the rest from the conversation, and it replaces both typing everything yourself and a meeting bot that hands you a generic recap.**

- "The AI notepad for back-to-back meetings" / "Notes, actions and memory." / "Without a meeting bot." VERIFIED, https://www.granola.ai/ (H1 and hero subhead).
- "An AI notepad works alongside you, not instead of you." VERIFIED, https://www.granola.ai/ai-note-taker
- What it replaces is ASSUMED from Granola's own contrast copy: "Most AI notetakers join your call as a bot, transcribe everything, and hand you a wall of text when it's over." VERIFIED, same page.

## Differentiator

**Your notes stay yours: Granola builds on what you wrote (your words in black, its additions in grey) and every line it adds can be traced back to the conversation.**

Evidence, all VERIFIED:
- "Granola is an AI notepad: it transcribes your meetings, keeps your notes yours," (https://www.granola.ai/ai-note-taker)
- "When the meeting ends, Granola combines what you jot down with what it captured, and produces notes that are actually useful." (same page)
- "Granola uses what you've written to shape its notes: any notes you take are seen as important details to include." (https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/taking-notes-in-granola)
- "If you took raw notes, they will be highlighted in black" (https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/ai-enhanced-notes)
- "Use the magnifying glass beside each note to see where in the transcript or raw notes the note came from" (same page)
- In the footage: black vs grey text explained at 41.2 to 57.5 s; the magnifying glass opening a "Transcript summary" popover at 67.1 to 78.0 s. VERIFIED from the contact sheets and transcript.

Why this one and not "no bot": the botless claim is Granola's loudest homepage line, but (a) it is not filmable from this clip, which only shows the after-meeting notes, and (b) it is no longer unique: Fathom's hero now says "Now available bot-free" (VERIFIED, https://www.fathom.ai/). The notes-stay-yours mechanic is both unique in Granola's own framing and visible on screen.

## Audience pains

Assumed audience (ASSUMED): people with back-to-back calls who have tried a bot-based recorder (Otter, Fireflies, Fathom) and found the recap generic or untrustworthy, or who still type notes and lose the conversation. Matches Granola's own "people in back-to-back meetings" and "ambitious, independent-minded, spinning too many plates" (VERIFIED, https://www.granola.ai/blog/a-new-look-for-granola).

In their words (VERIFIED, Product Hunt reviews, https://www.producthunt.com/products/granola/reviews):
- "I used to spend so much time taking notes during meetings" (Taissa Maleh)
- "No more Ai Notetakers getting added to meetings" (Jess Joyce)
- "a third participant with a robot avatar appears" (Asad M.)
- "The summary reads the same either way, so I go back to the transcript to check" (Asad M.)
- "My own typed notes staying interleaved with the transcript is the other half" (Asad M.)
- "It helps me stay focused during meetings instead of typing nonstop." (Riska Ambarastita)

The two pains this clip can answer on screen: "the summary reads the same either way" (answered by black vs grey: your notes are visibly yours) and "I go back to the transcript to check" (answered by the magnifying glass). The bot pain is real but the clip cannot show it.

## Previous version (SCRIPT.md v1): where its angle was weak or off-brand

What it got right: it picked the right differentiator ("Instead of replacing my notes, it builds on them." as the cold open), it calls Granola "an AI notepad", and it uses "AI note-takers" only as the foil, which matches Granola's own comparison page. The vocabulary is not the problem.

Where it was weak:
1. **Too long for the placement.** About 50 s against 15 s (X) and 15 to 30 s (LinkedIn) for a standalone post. Eight beats, three of them how-to.
2. **Beat 2 is generic AI-notes framing.** "Granola uses AI to review the entire conversation and transcript, and it also transformed my notes into a polished meeting summary." This is what Otter, Fireflies and Fathom all say (transcribe, summarize). It is sponsor-tutorial voice, not Granola's, and it spends 7.7 s restating what the cold open already said better.
3. **The proof beat proves the wrong thing.** Beats 3 and 4 (chocolate chips, sea salt, September 15th, "Three decisions in under 30 minutes?" with a kick on "30") prove the meeting was productive, not that Granola is different. "Under 30 minutes" is unverifiable and should not be accented as a stat. Every competitor claims action items; the real proofs here are black vs grey and the magnifying glass.
4. **The trust beat is told as instructions.** Beat 7's audio is "hover over and on the right hand side, I'll click on the magnifying glass", which is tutorial grammar. The beat is right (it answers "I go back to the transcript to check"); the line should be on-screen type, not the narrator's click-by-click.
5. **No brand until 47.5 s and stale brand assumptions.** The end card plan says "plain type, not their logo (we do not have the brand files)" and cut.yaml uses a dark `#141414` / lime `#9BD14A` palette. Both are now wrong: brand.md has official lockups and a light paper palette, and the footage is post-rebrand light UI. X guidance is brand within 3 s.
6. **No CTA and no category line.** It ends on "Granola" and the URL; Granola's own end is "Download for free" with the H1 category line.
7. **Silent viewing.** The story rides on the narrator; with sound off, the black vs grey idea needs an on-screen line, not just subtitles of his sentence.
8. **Rights not addressed** (see above), and the share link at 0 to 10 s was not flagged.
9. **"No bot" is absent without comment.** That is the clip's limit, not the script's fault, but the script should say the end card carries it.

## Three angles, ranked

1. **"Your notes, still yours."** Open on the finished note and let the grey lines visibly separate from your black ones (41 to 57 s), the one claim no competitor makes, shown rather than told; the end card then carries the category line and the botless subhead.
2. **"Check any line."** Answer the "I go back to the transcript to check" pain by following the magnifying glass from a grey line to its "Transcript summary" (67 to 78 s), framing Granola as the notes you can trust because every line has a receipt.
3. **"Admin, done."** Ring the three decisions and the two owners (24 to 34 s, 58 to 66 s) to sell post-meeting admin done; weakest, because action items are what every competitor already claims and the footage's decisions are about cookies.
