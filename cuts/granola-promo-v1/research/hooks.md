# Hooks: granola-promo-v1

Hook writer pass, 21 Oct 2026. Inputs: `research/brief.md`, `brand/brand.md`, `probe/Granola_AI_Tutorial_for_Beginners_206_291/` (transcript, word times, sheets 01 to 04, `grid_49.0.png`, `grid_75.5.png`), `docs/research/launch-craft.md` section 3 (library and rubric).

Context: standalone post on X and LinkedIn, 16:9, 20 to 30 s, nothing around it. So T (type card) hooks are allowed, but the owner's standing taste is product UI at frame 0, so V and C hooks are preferred and T hooks are here for spread.

## Constraints that shaped the scores

- **Readability math.** Body text in the tutorial's zoomed view (11.9 to 57 s and after the 57.5 s scroll) is about 8 px tall on the 640 px source. At a 360 px wide phone that is about 4.5 px, about 5.6 px at the 1.25x framing cap. No UI body line reads on a phone. What does read: the slab heading "Product Launch Planning", whole-block tone (black vs grey is visible as tone even when the words are not), and anything we re-set crisp in brand type. So for criterion A in this cut: **5 is unreachable**, 4 = UI fills the frame at 0 s and the key string is re-set crisp so it reads at 360 px, 3 = UI fills the frame but only the heading or a caption reads, 2 = frame 0 is type and the product arrives before 2 s.
- **Usable spans.** Nothing before 11.9 s (share link and extension nag to 10.5 s, then the tutorial's own zoom animation 10.9 to 11.9 s). End before 84.0 s. Every crop must exclude the footer fine print "Granola uses AI and can make mistakes." (source y about 355) and, where possible, the "Continue chat" bar (y about 320 to 335).
- **Clean frames.** 53.0 to 56.5 s: zoomed note, no selection, no "Quick edit" toolbar. 42.0 to 43.0 s the tutorial itself selects "Finalize the recipe" (a black line) as he says "black"; 44.0 to 46.5 s it selects the grey "Taste test feedback" lines as he says "gray"; 47.0 to 49.5 s "Decide on a launch date" (black); 50.0 to 52.5 s "Enough time for packaging..." and "Production begins next week" (grey). Magnifier: cursor reaches it 71.6 s, click 72.3 s, skeleton loader 72.5 to 73.3 s, text streams 73.4 to 74.3 s, popover held to 78.0 s. Typed edit "ideally by Tuesday" 81.5 to 83.5 s.
- **Claims.** Every claim below is either in the brief's allowed-claims table (cited by number) or visible in the footage. Re-set labels copy the recording's case: the popover reads "TRANSCRIPT SUMMARY" in caps. "Three decisions in under 30 minutes" is not used anywhere. Speaker naming is never implied to be automatic.
- **Rights.** Any hook that uses the narrator's voice depends on the Stratvert licence the brief flags as a blocker. Each hook says whether it survives the brief's fallback (type and music only, no narrator).

Spoken times below are word times from `transcript.json`, in source seconds.

## Top 3

### 1. H2: the labelled note ("What I wrote" / "Granola generated")
**Why it wins.** Frame 0 is the product, which is the owner's standing preference, and it is a finished poster: the slab heading plus two crisp labels. It shows the differentiator rather than saying it, in 5 words, from a clean frame (55.0 s) with no toolbar, link or fine print. It works muted and survives the no-narrator fallback by swapping to Granola's own "My notes" / "Enhanced".

**What it costs.**
- The hook is the payoff, so the cut cannot open on the promise and reveal later; it has to escalate. Proof 2 must be the magnifier (H3's beat, 71.6 to 74.3) so the second beat answers "can I trust the grey lines?".
- Grey text goes soft at 2x. The art director must check the grey line against the black one at 360 px; if the tone split does not read, re-set both lines crisp over the footage rather than ringing them.
- Brand needs to appear by 3 s for X: the slab heading helps, but add the spiral bug or put the lockup in the end card early enough.
- If the narrator is licensed, his 46.72 to 54.10 line follows naturally; do not caption "before the meeting" from it, Granola's docs speak of notes taken in the meeting.

### 2. H3: the magnifier click ("See where the note came from.")
**Why it wins.** The only real cause and effect in the clip that resolves inside 2 s: click at 0.7 s, popover streams in by about 1.8 s once the loader is trimmed. It answers a pain users write in their own words ("I go back to the transcript to check") and that competitors do not address. Muted-safe and fallback-safe.

**What it costs.**
- It opens on the second differentiator, so black vs grey (41 to 57 s) must still land before 10 s to meet the LinkedIn payoff rule and the brief's first angle.
- The skeleton loader at 72.5 to 73.3 s must be cut or ramped, and the narrator's audio there ("I'll click on the magnifying glass") is tutorial grammar, so this beat runs on SFX (click, pop) with no VO.
- Popover body text never reads at phone size. Re-set the header "TRANSCRIPT SUMMARY" in caps as recorded; do not imply the summary quotes the speaker by name automatically.
- The crop (source y about 40 to 328) brushes the "Continue chat" bar and must stay clear of the fine print at y about 355.

### 3. H14: copy becomes UI ("Decide on a launch date")
**Why it wins.** The most designed opening: a real line from the note, set large as the poster, pulling out into the app so the grey lines appear around it. It is H2's idea in launch-film grammar (output first, then the tool), suits Granola's editorial type, and is fallback-safe.

**What it costs.**
- Frame 0 is type, not UI, against the owner's product-first taste (A 3). Use it only if the director wants a more editorial film; otherwise H2.
- The pull-out is one camera move the whole cut must then match (P8): commit to the slow glide language throughout, no whips.
- Same grey-contrast check as H2, and the line must be set in a face that matches the app's body (Hanken Grotesk), not the display slab, so the landing is seamless.

Not in the top 3 but worth keeping: H1 is the strongest spoken line in the clip. If the Stratvert licence clears, use it as the VO under H2 at about 2 s, not as the opening.
