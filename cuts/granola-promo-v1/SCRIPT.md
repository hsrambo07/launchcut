## Cut on purpose

- Everything before 10.5 s of the source (live share link, extension nag) and everything after 83.74 s ("is.", black frames from 84.0).
- "Granola uses AI to review the entire conversation and transcript..." (15.76 to 23.32): generic AI-notes framing every competitor uses.
- The decisions and the joke (23.92 to 40.88), including "Three decisions in under 30 minutes?", which is unverifiable and not allowed.
- "You'll also notice that some of the text here is black..." and "The black text is what I wrote before the meeting..." (41.16 to 54.10): the labels say it in 5 words, and "before the meeting" is his claim, not Granola's (the docs talk about notes taken in the meeting).
- "Granola also labels who said what throughout the meeting." and the Responsibilities walk-through (57.80 to 66.40): implies automatic speaker naming, which the docs qualify.
- "here I could simply hover over and on the right hand side, I'll click on the magnifying glass," (69.66 to 73.78): tutorial grammar. The picture shows the click; the caption says why.

## Not claimed

- No "without a meeting bot" or botless claim: the clip cannot show it.
- No automatic speaker naming; Elizabeth's name is shown as it appears in the note.
- No "before the meeting", no meeting length, no "under 30 minutes".
- No pricing, accuracy, model names, platforms or integrations.
- "See where it came from." is an elision of allowed claim 6 ("Use the magnifying glass beside each note to see where in the transcript or raw notes the note came from"). The line turning black after an edit is shown, never stated as a rule.
- The end card line is the verbatim homepage H1 (allowed claim 7). "granola.ai" is the domain.
