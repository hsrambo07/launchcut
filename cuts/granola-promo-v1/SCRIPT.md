# Script: Granola promo (v1, about 50 s)

**Promotion context.** Granola is an AI notepad for meetings. You type rough notes while it transcribes the call; afterwards it "enhances" your notes into a full summary using the transcript. The idea that sells it is not "AI writes your notes" (everyone says that). It is that **it builds on the notes you wrote**, keeps your words in black and its additions in gray, catches the decisions and who owns what, and lets you check any line against the actual conversation.

**Audience.** People who live in back-to-back meetings and have tried AI note-takers that replace their notes with a generic recap.

**Arc.** Cold open on the differentiator, then how it works, then three proofs (decisions, yours vs Granola's, who said what), then the trust beat (receipts), then the name.

**Voice.** The tutorial narrator's own words, re-ordered. Nothing is re-recorded or invented; every line below is said in the source at the time shown.
